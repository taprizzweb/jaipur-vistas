Four reasons
your Jaipur trip
needs this table.

  1. 01

    A front-row seat to Hawa Mahal

    Our open rooftop on the 2nd & 3rd floor faces the Palace of Winds head-on. Walk in and the whole façade is right in front of you.

  2. 02

    Food guests travel back for

    Crispy-crust pizzas with real mozzarella, pesto mushroom sandwiches, pastas, Indian and Chinese favourites — fresh, tasty and fairly priced.

  3. 03

    Golden hour, every evening

    When the sun dips, the pink sandstone turns to gold — and after dark the palace lights up. Order a chai, sit back and watch.

  4. 04

    Made for memories (and reels)

    A checkerboard terrace, sculpted wave benches and the full palace in frame. Professional photography available on request.
