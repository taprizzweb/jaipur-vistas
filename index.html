Four reasons
your Jaipur trip
needs this table.

  1. 01

    A front-row seat to Hawa Mahal

    We sit right across the road from the Palace of Winds. No crowds, no tickets — just the most famous façade in Rajasthan, framed by your window.

  2. 02

    Royal Rajasthani on a plate

    Dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi, ker sangri and a sweet little ghewar — cooked the way Jaipur’s homes have cooked them for generations.

  3. 03

    Golden hour, every evening

    When the sun dips, the pink sandstone turns to gold. Order a kulhad chai, sit back and watch the old city glow.

  4. 04

    Made for memories (and reels)

    Jharokha frames, rooftop corners and a skyline that needs no filter. Every visit comes home as a photo.
