The heart behind the café

Jaipur’s colours.
Jaipur’s flavours.
Jaipur’s best view.

Jaipur Vista was born from a simple love for our city — the blue of its pottery, the pink of its walls, the peacocks of its palaces and the warmth of its people. We wanted one place where all of it comes together on a single table.

Between forts, palaces and bazaars, this is where you slow down. A cup of kulhad chai, a plate of something royal, and a view that makes you understand why the world falls in love with Jaipur.

Padharo mhare des — and stay a little longer.

Royal by heritage. Fresh by nature.

Fresh food.
Royal care.

At Jaipur Vista, every dish starts with fresh preparation, local Rajasthani ingredients and honest flavour — from ghee-roasted baatis to slow-cooked dals, made the way Jaipur’s homes have made them for generations.

Alongside, a modern café menu of coffees, shakes, pizzas and pastas, so everyone at the table — locals, families and travellers from across the world — finds a favourite.
